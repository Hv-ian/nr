Relocating to a new country is rarely a single decision. It is a sequence of them, and the order matters more than most people expect. This guide sets out the sequence we use with our own clients.

## Start with your legal basis

Before anything else, establish why you are entitled to live in Armenia. Most people arrive on one of a few grounds: employment, running a business, family reunification, or ethnic Armenian origin. Each carries its own documentation and its own timeline. Choosing the wrong basis is the single most common reason an application stalls.

Citizens of many countries can enter Armenia without a visa and stay for an extended period, which gives you room to prepare properly rather than rushing paperwork from abroad.

## Prepare documents before you arrive

Documents issued outside Armenia usually need translation into Armenian and, depending on the issuing country, an apostille. This is far easier to arrange while you are still in your home country. Birth certificates, marriage certificates, criminal record checks, and diplomas are the usual candidates.

## Register your status

Once your basis is settled and your documents are ready, the application itself is comparatively mechanical: submission, review by the relevant authority, and issuance of a residence card. Timelines vary with the grounds you applied under and the completeness of your file.

## Set up the practical layer

With status in hand, the rest follows in a predictable order:

- Obtain a tax identification number
- Open a local bank account
- Arrange health insurance
- Register a lease, if your grounds require a registered address
- Enrol children in school, if relevant

## Then settle in

The administrative part of relocation ends faster than the personal part. Learning where to shop, how to get a local SIM, which neighbourhood suits you, and a few dozen words of Armenian all take longer than any government procedure, and matter more to whether the move works.

> **Keep in mind:** Requirements and procedures can change. It is always best to check the latest information or get professional assistance.
