Starting November 1, 2026, significant changes to Armenia’s immigration legislation will come into effect. The new rules will affect residence permit application procedures, eligibility requirements, and state fees.

**What will change?**

**1. New Requirements for Obtaining a Residence Permit Through Business Activities**

Registering as an individual entrepreneur or establishing a company will no longer be sufficient on its own to qualify for a residence permit.

Applicants seeking a residence permit based on business activities will need to meet specific financial requirements:

▪️ **For company shareholders:** An investment of at least AMD 2 million in a company’s share capital or the acquisition of shares or securities of an equivalent value.

▪️ **For individual entrepreneurs:** A minimum balance of AMD 1 million in business bank accounts or business turnover of at least AMD 1 million during the 60 days preceding the application.

In addition, obtaining a permanent residence permit through business activities will require applicants to have held temporary residence permits for at least three years within the preceding five years.

**2. Increased State Fees**

From November 1, the applicable state fees will be:

▪️ **Temporary residence permit:** AMD 150,000 (previously AMD 105,000).

▪️ **Permanent residence permit:** AMD 250,000 (previously AMD 140,000).

▪️ **Certificate confirming lawful residence:** AMD 30,000 (previously issued free of charge).

If a residence permit application is rejected, the state fee paid will not be refunded. However, statutory exemptions and benefits relating to state fees will remain applicable.

**3. Transition to Electronic Applications**

Applications for residence permits on all grounds will be submitted through a unified electronic platform.

The platform will also be used for processing applications, exchanging documents, and notifying applicants.

**4. Changes to Residence Permit Categories**

The legislation establishes two main types of residence permits:

▪️ **Temporary residence permit:** Valid for up to 1 year.

▪️ **Permanent residence permit:** Valid for 5 years.

The separate special residence status will be abolished for new applicants. Special residence statuses issued previously will remain valid until their expiration dates.

**5. Introduction of Annual Residence Permit Quotas**

The new legislation also introduces a legal mechanism allowing annual quotas to be established for the issuance of residence permits.

**What Happens to Applications Submitted Before November 1?**

Applications submitted before November 1, 2026, will be reviewed under the rules in effect at the time of submission.

These changes are particularly important for foreign nationals planning to obtain residence permits through individual entrepreneurship or business activities in Armenia.
